About

This archive is a personal collection of chronologically sorted fansite images. Every photo is manually dated, though TWICE, ITZY, and some IVE images are still missing accurate dates.

I began archiving fansite photos of my favorite groups back in 2021 and have been collecting and organizing them ever since.

This project was built for fun, as a way to share a photo archiving hobby I started in my free time. I'm glad I could turn it into something easy to browse, and I hope others enjoy it as much as I do.

Enjoy browsing! ♡

If you’d like to help sort photos, report issues, or suggest improvements, message me on Discord @terril Copied! .
Layout may not display well on mobile.

Usage

  • Use the left sidebar to browse groups and select a member, or search for a specific name.
  • Once you're viewing a member's gallery, you can type a date into the search bar to quickly jump to the closest set of images from that day.
  • Use the random button to open a random image from the current gallery.
  • Click any image to open the lightbox and view the full resolution version, and use the download button to save it.
  • In the lightbox, hold Ctrl and click on an image to open the magnifier, then use your scroll wheel to zoom in and out.

Buttons

  • Sort by Date: Toggle between ascending/descending date order.
  • Chronological / Recent: Toggle sorting between manually assigned dates (chronological) or by the most recently saved images. Useful for seeing new additions even if they're from earlier dates.
  • Grid / Masonry: Toggle between uniform grid or natural aspect‑ratio layout.
